### Findings
Consensus: the fridge works for a weekly feed, and a fridge starter needs two or three room-temperature feedings, about 12 hours apart, before baking. Disagreement: King Arthur treats weekly fridge feeding as a routine, while Leo caps fridge storage at about three weeks and prefers slowing the feed (less water, smaller carryover) to refrigerating. King Arthur is internally inconsistent on room-temperature feeding (guide: twice a day; blog: daily). Expected absence: no source gives evidence on how starter microbiology changes in the cold.
